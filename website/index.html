Can Variational Quantum Classifiers Predict India's Stock Market?

Empirical evaluation of 4-Qubit and 8-Qubit Variational Quantum Classifiers (VQC) on NIFTY 50 direction prediction.

This project takes 10 years (2,706 days) of historical trading data from India's flagship stock index—the NIFTY 50—and translates daily price action into quantum states using ZZFeatureMap entanglement gates and a trainable RealAmplitudes variational ansatz. Evaluated on a strictly untouched 2025 test set, we test whether quantum algorithms can break the limits of classical machine learning or if the Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH) holds true.

🎯
The Financial Objective

Predict whether tomorrow's NIFTY 50 closing price will be HIGHER (UP) or LOWER (DOWN) than today's close, using 8 engineered technical indicators.

BINARY CLASSIFICATION: UP vs DOWN
⚛️
The Quantum Mechanism

Classical indicators are reduced via PCA into 4 principal components, mapped to [0, π] radians, and rotated across 4 entangled qubits in a 16-dimensional complex Hilbert space.

ZZFeatureMap + RealAmplitudes + SPSA
🔬
The Scientific Verdict

On the 2025 holdout set, the 4-Qubit VQC achieved 48.39% and 8-Qubit achieved 50.81% accuracy. McNemar testing (p > 0.50) confirms statistical parity with classical ML.

EMH VERIFIED • NO OVERFITTING

🌌 QUANTUM THEORETICAL FOUNDATIONS

Physics & Financial Theory

A beginner-friendly, accessible breakdown of Quantum Machine Learning (QML), Hilbert space mathematics, near-term NISQ limitations, and Eugene Fama's Efficient Market Hypothesis.

01

Bits vs Qubits

While classical bits are constrained to discrete binary states (0 or 1), a quantum bit (qubit) exists in a continuous linear superposition of states. This enables simultaneous evaluation of multiple basis states in parallel.

|ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩   [|α|² + |β|² = 1]
02

Quantum Entanglement

Two or more qubits can become inextricably linked such that the state of one cannot be described independently of the other. In our VQC, entanglement is generated via RZZ two-qubit interaction gates, establishing non-linear relationships across technical financial features.

RZZ(θ) = exp(−i θ/2 · Z ⊗ Z)
03

Quantum Feature Mapping

To process classical data on a quantum device, input vectors x ∈ ℝ4 are transformed into statevectors |ψ(x)⟩ in a 16-dimensional complex Hilbert space ℂ16 using Hadamard rotations and phase rotations RZ(2xi).

Φ : ℝ4 → ℂ16   [UΦ(x)|0⟩⊗4]
04

Variational Quantum Ansatz

The parameterized quantum circuit V(θ) serves as the quantum counterpart to classical neural network layers. It applies trainable single-qubit RY rotations interleaved with Controlled-Z entangling gates to adjust quantum probability amplitudes.

V(θ) = ∏ [CZ · RY(θi)]   [θ ∈ ℝ8]
05

SPSA Noise-Resilient Optimizer

Traditional backpropagation fails on quantum systems due to quantum measurement collapse and noisy cost landscapes. SPSA approximates gradients by simultaneously perturbing all parameters randomly in two directions, requiring only 2 circuit runs per iteration.

gk(θ) ≈ [L(θ + ckΔk) − L(θ − ckΔk)] / (2ckΔk)
06

Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH)

Proposed by Eugene Fama (1970), EMH establishes that financial market prices rapidly incorporate all available information. Short-term daily price movements follow near-random walks. This explains why both classical models and quantum VQC achieve ~50% directional accuracy.

𝔼[ Rt+1 | ℐt ] = 0   (Martingale / Random Walk)
