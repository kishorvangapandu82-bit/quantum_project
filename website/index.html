💡 Key Research Findings
1
No quantum advantage detected. VQC (48.39%) performs below all classical baselines under 4-qubit NISQ simulation. Shallow circuits on noisy financial time-series do not outperform classical methods.
2
All models near random chance. With 50% class balance on 2025 test set, a baseline guesser achieves 50%. LR and SVM hit exactly 50% — barely above random. Best classical: RF at 52.42%.
3
VQC systematic UP-day bias. VQC learned the positive drift of NIFTY 50 in training (2015–2022: 53.49% UP). This leads to Recall=86.29% but Specificity=8.06% — it almost always predicts UP.
4
Statistical noise dominates. McNemar p-values range 0.56–0.71. At n=248, no pair is distinguishable at any conventional significance level (p<0.05, p<0.10, p<0.20).
5
PCA compression is efficient. 4 PCs retain 94.89% of variance across 8 technical indicators — near-lossless compression for quantum encoding.

⚠️ Study Limitations
!
4-qubit constraint: Forces PCA compression, stripping some high-frequency signals from the feature space.
!
Simulation only: Not tested on real quantum hardware. Physical QPU noise would further degrade performance.
!
Limited features: Only 8 technical indicators. Fundamental data, macro factors, and sentiment excluded.
!
No trading strategy: Pure classification accuracy study — no transaction costs, no portfolio backtest.
